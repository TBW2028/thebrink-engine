Two recent readings.

The same signal can matter a lot or very little. Here is how we read two events from the past.

Flash flooding across Tunis, Tunisia

17 September 2026

Severe thunderstorms and heavy downpours flooded main roads and residential areas and left vehicles stranded across the city.

What it could mean

Road access is the immediate problem. For a site or supplier nearby, the questions are drainage, staff access and whether deliveries slip over the next few days.

M5.5 earthquake, Kermadec Islands region

18 September 2026, 23:18 UTC, depth 94 km

A moderate earthquake, recorded deep below the seabed in the southwest Pacific.

What it could mean

Events this deep and this size are usually felt widely but do little surface damage. It becomes worth watching for Pacific shipping and island infrastructure if further quakes cluster nearby.

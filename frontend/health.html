AVIAN INFLUENZA (H5N1)

No Raw Dairy or Undercooked Poultry

Do not consume unpasteurized raw milk or raw/runny eggs. Avoid live bird markets, domestic poultry culls, and contact with dead wild fowl.

Active Corridor: US Dairy Belts, EU Poultry Clusters, East Asia

CHOLERA & ENTERIC INFECTIONS

Mandatory Water Boiling & Sterilization

Boil all drinking water vigorously for >1 minute or use certified RO filtration. Never consume cut roadside fruits, raw salads, or untreated ice.

Active Corridor: South Asia (Maharashtra/Gujarat/Assam), Horn of Africa
Timeline: Active Monsoon/Flood Window (June through October)

Everyday Precautions for Common People & Families
ADAPTIVE SEASONAL DIRECTIVES

1. Stagnant Water Checks

Every Sunday, empty and scrub clean all flowerpot saucers, AC drainage trays, and bucket storage. Aedes mosquitoes that spread Dengue only require a bottle cap of clean standing water to breed.

2. Drinking Water Safety

Municipal pipelines frequently cross near open stormwater drains. If rainwater seeps into supply lines, boil drinking water for at least 1 full minute, even if water looks clear.

3. Fever Management

Never take pain relievers like Ibuprofen or Aspirin for sudden high fever during monsoon seasons, as they can cause severe bleeding if the fever is Dengue. Use Paracetamol and consult a doctor.

1. Masking in Poorly Ventilated Areas

Winter temperature inversions trap airborne particulates and respiratory droplets. Wear an N95 mask in crowded morning commute trains, metro cars, and enclosed clinic waiting rooms.

2. Annual Flu Vaccination

High-risk individuals (senior citizens, diabetic patients, and young children) should receive their annual quadrivalent influenza booster before seasonal temperature drops begin in November.

3. Hand Hygiene Routine

Cold-weather respiratory viruses survive longer on doorknobs, elevator buttons, and shared touch surfaces. Wash hands with soap for 20 seconds before touching eyes, nose, or mouth.

1. Food Spoilage Prevention

Bacterial growth accelerates rapidly above 32°C. Refrigerate cooked food within 1 hour of preparation. Avoid mayonnaise, raw dairy desserts, and unrefrigerated meat dishes.

2. Electrolyte & Heat Stroke Defense

Drink water mixed with oral rehydration salts (ORS) or coconut water when outdoor temperatures exceed seasonal baselines. Avoid peak sun exposure between 12:00 PM and 3:30 PM.

3. Eye & Waterborne Conjunctivitis

Avoid public swimming pools without verified chlorine levels during early summer viral waves. Do not share towels or eye drops with family members showing red or itchy eyes.
